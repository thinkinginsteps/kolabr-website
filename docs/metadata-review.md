# Metadata review

Revised 23 September 2026 for search intent and applied to `lib/page-meta.ts`. Every title is 50
to 60 characters, every description 140 to 155, no em dashes, and the copy rules are kept.

## What changed, and why

1. **The search phrase leads, the brand comes last.** Titles read `Kolabr | thing` before. Nobody
   searches for Kolabr yet, so the first and most heavily weighted words were spent on a word with
   no demand. They now read `thing | Kolabr`.
2. **Use case pages target how readers describe their own problem**, for example "client
   collaboration software for law firms" rather than "Kolabr for law firms".
3. **Compare pages target alternative searches.** Many more people search "slack alternative" than
   any "X vs Y" phrase for a brand they have never heard of, so the title leads with that and keeps
   the head to head after the pipe.
4. **Descriptions lead with the concrete thing** (a channel per matter, per client, per site) and
   name the differentiator, free guests, because that is what earns the click.

Legal pages are unchanged and stay noindex.
