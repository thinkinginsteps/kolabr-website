campaign From 1 September the customer is notified of a non-conformance within four hours, not at the end of the investigation. The steps below reflect that: a late notification is a bigger finding than the defect.

What to do the moment a part measures out of tolerance: how far back to quarantine, who to tell and in what order, and how the 8D gets opened. Written for quality and production staff on shift.

Before you start

Confirm the measurement on a second gauge before you stop a line. A calibration drift reported as a defect costs a shift. If the parts have already been fitted by the customer, this is not the right page. Escalate to the plant manager immediately. Nothing suspect ships on a concession without written customer approval.
