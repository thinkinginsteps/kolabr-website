Cookie policy

What [COMPANY NAME], trading as Kolabr, stores on your device, why, and how to control it.

Last updated 21 September 2026 Applies to kolabr.com and the Kolabr apps
On this page 1. What cookies are 2. What we use, and why 3. Third parties 4. Controlling cookies 5. The mobile apps 6. Changes to this policy

In short

We use the cookies needed to log you in and keep the service secure, plus a small amount of privacy-respecting analytics. No advertising cookies, no third-party trackers, and nothing that follows you around other websites.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device so it can recognise you on your next request. Related technologies do much the same job: local storage keeps settings in your browser, and session storage forgets them when you close the tab. We use all three, and refer to them together as cookies here.

2. What we use, and why

Strictly necessary

These make the service work. They cannot be switched off without breaking sign-in, and they do not require consent.

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are read and where people get stuck. It is aggregated, it does not build a profile of you, and it is not shared with advertisers.

What we do not use

No advertising cookies. No retargeting pixels. No social media trackers. No cross-site tracking, and no selling of behavioural data to anyone.

3. Third parties

A small number of providers set cookies when their functionality is used: our payment processor during checkout, and our analytics and error-monitoring providers. Each is contractually limited to processing data on our instructions, and none of them is permitted to use it for advertising.

Video calls started from a channel may set a cookie for the duration of the call to hold media settings. It expires when the call ends.

4. Controlling cookies

When you first visit our website you can accept or decline analytics cookies. You can change that choice at any time from the link in the website footer, and declining does not degrade the service.

Your browser also lets you block or delete cookies:

  • Chrome: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies.
  • Safari: Settings, then Privacy.
  • Firefox: Settings, then Privacy and Security.
  • Edge: Settings, then Cookies and site permissions.

Blocking strictly necessary cookies will stop you being able to sign in, because there is no other way for the service to know who you are between one request and the next.

5. The mobile apps

The iOS and Android apps do not use browser cookies. They store an authentication token and your preferences in the device’s secure storage, and clear both when you sign out or remove the app. They contain no advertising identifiers.

6. Changes to this policy

If we add or remove a cookie we will update the tables above and the date at the top of this page. Where a new cookie requires consent we will ask for it before setting it.

More detail on how we handle personal information is in the privacy policy.
